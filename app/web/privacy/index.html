Four Questions

Privacy

Last updated 8 October 2026

Four Questions is a small family app: a few questions, once a day, with the answers kept in one Google Sheet the family shares. It has no server of its own. Everything you do in it happens in your browser, between you and Google.

What the app asks Google for

  • Your name and email address (the openid, email and profile permissions), to know who is answering and to show it in the app.
  • Access to Google Sheets (the spreadsheets permission). The app uses it for one spreadsheet only, the family's Four Questions sheet: it reads the questions and answers there and writes your answers and the name you choose. It does not open, list or change any other spreadsheet. Google's permission is broader than that only because Sheets offers no narrower one for a sheet the app did not create.

You can use the app only if the sheet's owner has shared it with your Google account as an editor.

Where your data goes

  • Into the family sheet. Your answers, notes and chosen name are saved there, next to your email address, where everyone the sheet is shared with can see them.
  • On your device. The app keeps your name, email, a short-lived Google access token, a copy of the questions and recent answers, and any answers not yet saved, in your browser's local storage, so it opens quickly and works offline. Signing out of the app removes them.
  • Nowhere else. There are no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no other services. Your data is not sold, shared with anyone outside the sheet, or used for anything but the app itself. The site is hosted on Google Firebase Hosting, which, like any web host, keeps standard request logs.

Google user data

Four Questions' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Sign out in the app to clear what it keeps about you on your device.
  • Remove the app's access at any time in your Google Account, under Third-party apps & services.
  • To have your answers or name removed from the sheet, ask the sheet's owner, or email the address below.
